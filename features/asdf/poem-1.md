# Digital Dawn

Lines of code cascade like morning light,
Each function a brushstroke in the night,
Variables dance in memory's embrace,
While algorithms find their perfect place.

The compiler hums its ancient song,
Transforming thoughts where they belong,
From human mind to silicon dream,
A symphony of logic's gleam.

Bugs may lurk in shadows deep,
But tests stand guard while systems sleep,
And when the build at last runs true,
The world awakens, born anew.
