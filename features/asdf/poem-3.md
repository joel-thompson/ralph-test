## City Pulse

Neon signs flicker against concrete walls,
their colors bleeding into puddles
where yesterday's rain still lingers.

The subway rumbles beneath our feet—
a heartbeat in the city's chest,
carrying strangers to nowhere and everywhere.

Street vendors call out in languages
I don't understand,
but their meaning is clear:
hunger knows no borders.

A saxophone wails from an alley,
notes climbing fire escapes
to windows where someone
might be listening,
or might be alone.

The traffic light changes.
We move.
We stop.
We move again,
a choreography we've memorized
without ever learning the steps.
